# Unique Features

Some example *Unique Features* (class advancements at 3<sup>rd</sup>, 6<sup>th</sup>, etc) appear below. Each may be selected once only, unless the GM determines otherwise. “Crossclass” feats are only available to PCs of a different class.

Most UFs include three tiers, representing low, moderate and high power versions. Each tier accumulates upon the last. The GM decides which power level UFs operate at in their campaign (either globally or on a per ability basis, bearing in mind the specific PCs involved), or might allow players to cherry pick benefits from the list.


Tables should in no way feel constrained by these examples. GMs and players are encouraged to customise what follows to suit their game, and/or use them as guides for their own creations.

## Adaptable (Crossclass)

You gain a limited version of the Fighter *Adaptable* ability. Learn two fighting styles (you do not gain a default style). You begin an adventure with two uses of this ability.

## Alchemy & Mechanica (Crossclass)

You gain a limited version of the Artificer *Alchemy & Mechanica* ability. Gain one invention. You begin an adventure with two uses of this ability (if applicable).

## Armour Master

<table border="1">
<tr><td><b>1</b></td><td>While wearing armour, you reduce all damage suffered by 2 points.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>2</b></td><td>You may spend a <i>Reroll</i> die to reduce the damage of an attack by your level.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>3</b></td><td>The PC may make a <i>Luck</i> save to negate a single physical attack that reduces them to zero hp (once only, then the armour requires repair, similar to a shield p.62).</td></tr>
</table>

</table>

## Combat Medic

<table border="1">
<tr><td><b>1</b></td><td>During a short rest, you may automatically restore half of a touched target’s lost hit points (or your own). The target may not benefit from other short rest healing during the same rest. This ability requires access to a healer’s kit. You begin an adventure with one use of this ability.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>2</b></td><td>Once per adventure, you may spend an action to grant a touched target (or yourself) another <i>Luck</i> save vs ongoing poison or disease.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>3</b></td><td>Once per adventure, after combat, you may grant a touched target (or yourself) advantage on a check or save to avoid permanently losing a limb/organ.</td></tr>
</table>

## Dance of Dragons

<table border="1">
<tr><td><b>1</b></td><td>If you are unarmoured (and not grabbed or overly encumbered) add your Will bonus to your AC.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>2</b></td><td>In response to being hit by a ranged attack, spend a <i>Reroll</i> die to immediately gain up to three quarters cover, possibly turning the hit into a miss (no action, but there must be sufficient cover within 10 ft).</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>3</b></td><td>If you make a successful <i>Luck</i> (Dex) save, and would normally take half damage, you may spend a <i>Reroll</i> die to take no damage instead.</td></tr>
</table>

## Divine Blessings (Crossclass)

You gain a limited version of the Cultist *Blessings* ability. Choose a god, gain its tenets (not Patron Benefits) and learn two blessings. You begin an adventure with two uses of this ability.

## Elementalist

<table border="1">
<tr><td><b>1</b></td><td>Once per adventure, you may spend an action to magically control an element within 30 ft, generating one of the below spell effects:<br/><b>Air:</b> <i>Thunderwave</i> (wave form).<br/><b>Earth/Stone:</b> <i>Shield</i>.<br/><b>Fire:</b> <i>Fire Lash</i> (as <i>Magic Missile</i>, but made of fire and may also target objects, in which case a ranged attack roll is required to hit a moving object).<br/><b>Water:</b> <i>Bloodbound</i> (as <i>Hold Person</i>, but single target only).</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>2</b></td><td>You may use this ability twice per adventure.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>3</b></td><td>You may use this ability three times per adventure.</td></tr>
</table>

## Fame or Infamy

<table border="1">
<tr><td><b>1</b></td><td>When you enter an outpost or village, you may make a Charisma check. On a great success, at least some of the residents recognise you, and have heard of your past deeds (choose at least one deed). The GM has right of veto for remote locations.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>2</b></td><td>When you enter a town, you may make a Charisma check. On a great success, a significant number of the residents recognise you, and have heard of your past deeds (choose at least one deed). The GM has right of veto for remote locations.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>3</b></td><td>When you enter a city quarter, you may make a Charisma check. On a great success, at least some of the residents recognise you, and have heard of your past deeds (along with those of close allies; choose at least one deed). The GM has a general right of veto for cities.</td></tr>
</table>

## Faustian Pact

<table border="1">
<tr><td><b>1</b></td><td>You have a magical pact with an immortal being (non-deity) beyond the Veil. Once per adventure, in exchange for a price or debt brokered with the GM, you may spend an action to invoke a spell of 2<sup>nd</sup> level or lower (cast at your level). If no agreement can be reached, your action is wasted.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>2</b></td><td>You may invoke a spell of 3<sup>rd</sup> level, at the additional cost of 1 <i>Luck</i> point.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>3</b></td><td>When you use this ability, instead of a spell, you may invoke a supernatural effect that operates like a Rescue Exploit (no action, negotiate a price/debt as normal). The usual Dex and <i>Luck</i> checks (p.75) are required. The Rescue may target yourself if you wish.</td></tr>
</table>

## Ferocious Rage (Crossclass)

You gain a limited version of the Barbarian *Ferocious Rage* ability. You may rage only when *Staggered*, and do not gain +2 to Str/Con checks, to hit, damage or *Luck* saves. You begin an adventure with two uses of this ability.

## Signature Weapon

<table border="1">
<tr><td><b>1</b></td><td>Choose one weapon type. While using such treat your damage dice as exploding (ie if you roll the highest value, roll again and accumulate).</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>2</b></td><td>Once per adventure, while using your signature weapon, you may perform a <b>Major Exploit</b> without reducing your <i>Luck</i>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>3</b></td><td>Once per adventure, while using your signature weapon, you may spend a <i>Reroll</i> die to turn a missed attack into a hit instead.</td></tr>
</table>

## Skill Prodigy

<table border="1">
<tr><td><b>1</b></td><td>Gain a new skill or choose one you already know. Gain advantage whenever making checks related to that skill.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>2</b></td><td>Your great success range when using the skill increases by 2 (halve the attribute, then add 2).</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>3</b></td><td>If you do fail a relevant skill check, the GM may choose to award a partial success, or success at a cost, instead.</td></tr>
</table>

## Slippery Bastard

<table border="1">
<tr><td><b>1</b></td><td>Gain a +3 bonus when attempting to escape bonds, manacles, webs, nets, being grabbed, etc, and <i>Luck</i> saves for <i>Party Retreats</i>.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>2</b></td><td>When you are hit by a free attack due to your movement, you may spend a <i>Reroll</i> die to force the attacker to reroll. You must accept the result of the new roll.</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>3</b></td><td>Subject to GM veto, you may attempt escape even in situations where escape would normally be impossible (eg: by spitting up a tiny piece of lockpicking wire concealed in a false tooth, extreme body contortion, manipulating double jointed shoulders/elbows/wrists/knees, etc).</td></tr>
</table>

## Slow Burner

<tr><td><b>2</b></td><td>On a natural 1 attack roll, restore a <i>Reroll</i> die (max once per short rest).</td></tr>
<tr><td><b>3</b></td><td>While <i>Staggered</i>, gain adv on all <i>Luck</i> saves.</td></tr>
</table>

## Spell Casting (Crossclass)

You gain a limited version of the Magic User *Spell Casting* ability. Learn two 1<sup>st</sup> level spells. You begin an adventure with two 1<sup>st</sup> level spell uses of this ability, and a DDM tally of 4.

<table border="1">
<tr><td><b>1</b></td><td>Normal animals under your care (max Cha bonus, +1 per odd level) gain advantage on morale checks, and are treated as PCs for injuries, healing and death. If you direct your animals to return home or to camp, etc, they automatically find their way.</td></tr>

</table>
