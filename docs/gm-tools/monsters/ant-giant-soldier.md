# Ant, Giant (Soldier)
**No. Appearing:** 2d3<br>
**Armour Class:** 15<br>
**Hit Dice:** 3+3<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 2d4 + poison<br>
**Nat 19:** the soldier spits acid on the target, causing 2d4 damage.<br>
**Attributes:** S:19 D:12 C:16 I:1 P:12 W:12 Ch:8<br>
**Luck:** 6<br>
**Move:** 40 ft and may climb walls, ceilings, etc<br>

Soldier ants are approximately 4 ft long with a venomous bite that requires a *Luck* (Con) save or the target loses 1d4 Con. Soldiers may swarm a target, gaining +1 to hit for each additional ant beyond the first (maximum +4).
