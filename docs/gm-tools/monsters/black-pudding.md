# Black Pudding
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 13<br>
**Hit Dice:** 9<br>
**Attacks:** Pseudopod 2d8 or Fling Corrosive Glob 2d8 (up to 60 ft).<br>
**Nat 19:** the target's weapon is corroded, imposing a -2 penalty to hit until repaired.<br>
**Attributes:** S:19 D:8 C:16 I:2 P:14 W:16 Ch:1<br>
**Luck:** 10<br>
**Move:** 30 ft and may climb walls, ceilings, etc<br>

Black puddings are amorphous blobs composed of a very sticky and corrosive sludge. They are subterranean predators and scavengers. Non-magical weapons or armour making contact with a black pudding must make a *Luck* save or be destroyed, eaten away by the fast acting acid. Black puddings are immune to cold damage and split in half if struck by lightning (halve hit points). Black puddings make *Off Turn Attacks*.
