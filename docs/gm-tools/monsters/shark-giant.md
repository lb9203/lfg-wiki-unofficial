# Shark, Giant
**No. Appearing:** 2d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 12<br>
**Hit Dice:** 5+3<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 3d6<br>
**Nat 19:** the target must make a *Luck* (Con) save or lose a limb.<br>
**Attributes:** S:18 D:13 C:- I:2 P:8 W:10 Ch:4<br>
**Luck:** 8<br>
**Move:** 60 ft swimming<br>

The above statistics are for 15 ft, aggressive man eating giant sharks. When a giant shark reduces a target to *Staggered*, all giant sharks in a 20 ft radius go into a feeding frenzy, gaining a free attack.
