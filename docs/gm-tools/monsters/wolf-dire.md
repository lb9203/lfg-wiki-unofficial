# **Wolf, Dire**
**No. Appearing:** 2d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 13<br>
**Hit Dice:** 3+4<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 2d4+1<br>
**Nat 19:** if appropriate, a wolf (60%) or dire wolf (40%) comes to aid the dire wolf.<br>
**Attributes:** S:18 D:15 C:17 I:2 P:13 W:14 Ch:6<br>
**Luck:** 6<br>
**Move:** 60 ft<br>

Dire wolves are horse sized, cruel and malevolent wolves with a taste for human flesh. They have advantage when tracking and are sometimes used as steeds. If dire wolves flank their prey, they gain advantage on attack rolls instead of +1. Some rare corrupted dire wolves may issue an eldritch howl once/day, raising any dead wolves or dire wolves within 120 ft as obedient zombies in one round.
