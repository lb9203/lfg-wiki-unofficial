# Ogre Mage
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 15<br>
**Hit Dice:** 7+4<br>
**Attacks:** Big Sword 2d8<br>
**Nat 19:** the Ogre Mage summons spirit warriors equivalent to a *Monster Summoning II* spell.<br>
**Attributes:** S:19 D:10 C:15 I:15 P:14 W:15 Ch:10<br>
**Luck:** 9<br>
**Move:** 40 ft---<br>

The ogre mage is a highly intelligent ogre with spell casting abilities. An ogre mage may cast *Fly*, *Invisibility*, *Darkness 15' Radius*, *Sleep*, *Charm Person*, *Phantasmal Force* and *Cone of Cold* once per day. Ogre mages may spend an action to assume human shape or shift back to their real form. If an ogre mage rolls a natural 19-20 attack, the target must make a *Luck* (Con) save or roll on the *Injuries & Setbacks* table.
