# Aberrant Terror, Gibbering
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 14<br>
**Hit Dice:** 8<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 2d8<br>
**Nat 19:** the aberration unleashes a disturbing howl, causing all targets within 20 ft to make a *Luck* (Will) save or suffer a moderate madness.<br>
**Attributes:** S:16 D:7 C:16 I:3 P:10 W:14 Ch:1<br>
**Luck:** 9<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

Any intelligent being within 60 ft of a gibbering terror must make a *Luck* (Will) save. On a fail, the target either gains a random moderate madness (60%) or loses 1 point Will (40%).
