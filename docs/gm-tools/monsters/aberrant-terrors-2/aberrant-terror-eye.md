# Aberrant Terror, Eye
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 17<br>
**Hit Dice:** 12<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 3d6, plus 1d6 eye stalks.<br>
**Nat 19:** The aberration makes a free Bite attack against a single melee target. If it hits, the target loses a limb (*Luck* (Dex) save resists).<br>
**Attributes:** S:14 D:12 C:14 I:18 P:19 W:16 Ch:1<br>
**Luck:** 12<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

Each turn an eye terror makes one Bite attack and uses 1d6 eye stalks to project magical beams of energy that mimic spells. Roll 1d8: (1) *Disintegrate*, (2) *Magic Missile*, (3) *Flesh to Stone*, (4) *Frighten* (5) *Sleep*, (6) *Charm Person*, (7) *Hold Monster*, (8) *Blindness*). Additionally, at no action cost, an eye terror's central eye may use *Dispel Magic* in a 60 ft cone with a 30 ft base once per round.
