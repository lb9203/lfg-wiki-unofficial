# Demon, Lemure
**No. Appearing:** 4d10<br>
**Armour Class:** 12<br>
**Hit Dice:** 3<br>
**Attacks:** Claw 1d4+1 and special<br>
**Nat 19:** the demon's touch imparts dark sorcery; the target rolls on the *Dark & Dangerous Magic* table.<br>
**Attributes:** S:10 D:10 C:16 I:7 P:8 W:12 Ch:7<br>
**Luck:** 6<br>
**Move:** 20 ft<br>

Lemures are vaguely humanoid, but their flesh is clay like, shifting and malleable upon their grotesque bodies. They are lower forms of demons and feed on lost souls. A lemur's touch drains 1 Cha (a *Luck* (Will) save resists). An adventurer reduced to zero Cha dies and is transformed into a Lemure in 1d4 hours. Lemures have 25% *Magic Resistance*.
