# Demon, Manipede
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 14<br>
**Hit Dice:** 9+2<br>
**Attacks:** 2d4 Paralyzing Caress 1d4 + special, and Bite 1d8+1<br>
**Nat 19:** a random limb is paralysed for 1d6 months (*Luck* (Con) save resists).<br>
**Attributes:** S:14 D:17 C:10 I:7 P:13 W:15 Ch:6<br>
**Luck:** 10<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

Manipedes are 7 ft, gaunt and gangly humanoid demons with eight arms. Like all demons, they are corruption incarnate, despising all that lives, eager to slay and devour souls to prolong their manifestation in the physical world. On a hit, the target must make a *Luck* (Con) save or be paralysed for 2d6 rounds. Manipedes are protected by 50% *Magic Resistance*.
