# Giant, Hill
**No. Appearing:** 1d12<br>
**Armour Class:** 12<br>
**Hit Dice:** 8+2<br>
**Attacks:** Oversized Club 2d8+2<br>
**Nat 19:** special<br>
**Attributes:** S:20 D:8 C:15 I:6 P:9 W:12 Ch:8<br>
**Luck:** 9<br>
**Move:** 40 ft<br>

Hill giants are approximately 12 ft tall, the lowest caste of the true giants. Most are primitive cave dwellers or nomads with only rudimentary language, carrying clubs and crude tools. They hurl boulders, carts, barrels or humanoidcreatures up to 120 ft for 3d6 damage. Hill giants have an acute sense of smell, gaining advantage on related checks.
