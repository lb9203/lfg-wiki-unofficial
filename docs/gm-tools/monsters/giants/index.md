# Giants

Giants are at least 12 ft tall, massive and frightening humanoids filled with arrogance, avarice and ire. Giants are strict carnivores and consider humans a preferred delicacy compared to cows or sheep. Creatures of less than 2 HD must make a *Luck (Will)* save or flee for 2d6 rounds upon sighting a giant. All giants *Cause Injuries* on a natural 19-20 attack roll and have a 10 ft reach.
