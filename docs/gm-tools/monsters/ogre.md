# Ogre
**No. Appearing:** 1, a mated pair, or 2d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 12<br>
**Hit Dice:** 4+3<br>
**Attacks:** Spiked Club 2d8<br>
**Nat 19:** special<br>
**Attributes:** S:19 D:8 C:15 I:6 P:7 W:8 Ch:7<br>
**Luck:** 7<br>
**Move:** 40 ft<br>

Ogres are 9 ft, hairless, powerfully built humanoids, with elongated canines and pointed ears. They are of low intelligence, brutal and easy to anger. When *Staggered*, an ogre enters a murderous rage, gaining a free attack against every foe within reach. If an ogre rolls a natural 19-20 attack, the target must make a *Luck* (Con) save or roll on the *Injuries & Setbacks* table. Ogres have a 10 ft reach.
