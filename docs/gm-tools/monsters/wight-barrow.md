# **Wight, Barrow**
**No. Appearing:** 2d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 14<br>
**Hit Dice:** 5<br>
**Attacks:** Claw 1d6 hp + level drain<br>
**Nat 19:** the wight's touch imparts dark sorcery; the target rolls on the *Dark & Dangerous Magic* table.<br>
**Attributes:** S:17 D:14 C:14 I:14 P:16 W:15 Ch:8<br>
**Luck:** 8<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

Barrow wights are semi decayed humanoid *Undead* that inhabit tombs, graveyards, and burial mounds (barrows). They are immune to non-magical weapons, except those of cold iron, which affect them normally. Barrow wights possess a cursed insight into forbidden mysteries, reflected in their unhinged and sadistic personalities. All barrow wights develop some degree of spell casting ability, equal to 1d4+1 magic user levels. A barrow wight's claw attack drains one level from the victim (a *Luck* (Will) save resists) until the end of the adventure. An adventurer reduced to zero levels rises as an obedient zombie in 1d4 hours.
