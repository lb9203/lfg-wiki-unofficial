# Ettin
**No. Appearing:** 1d6<br>
**Armour Class:** 13<br>
**Hit Dice:** 10+3<br>
**Attacks:** 2 x Big Clubs 2d10<br>
**Nat 19:** the target is knocked prone and mocked mercilessly by both heads.<br>
**Attributes:** S:19 D:10 C:15 I:7 P:9 W:12 Ch:8<br>
**Luck:** 10<br>
**Move:** 40 ft<br>

Ettins are 12 ft Neanderthal like humanoids with two heads. They are relatively dull witted and the heads often argue with each other. With two brains ettins have advantage on any mind affecting *Luck* save. Ettins *Cause Injuries* on critical hits and have a 10 ft reach. On a fumbled attack the ettin heads break into a violent argument and lose their next turn.
