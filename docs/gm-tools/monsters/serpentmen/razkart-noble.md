# **Razkart (Noble)**
**No. Appearing:** 1d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 18<br>
**Hit Dice:** 9+4<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 1d6 + poison, or weapon<br>
**Nat 19:** the *razkart* unleashes a potent charm effect; the target makes a *Luck* (Will) save or is subject to the equivalent of a *Confusion* spell.<br>
**Attributes:** S:12 D:19 C:14 I:18 P:16 W:18 Ch:14<br>
**Luck:** 10<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

*Razkart* are the noble caste of the serpentmen, ruling over their civilization with great cunning and ruthlessness. They seek to restore the serpentfolk to greatness, or in the alternative, to hasten humankind towards final destruction. The *razkart* often masquerade as humans, seeking positions of power with access to networks of spies and informants. *Razkart* take great pleasure in manipulating foolish warmbloods as their plans unfold. *Razkart* may spend an action to shapeshift between human, noble and hybrid form. Some are powerful sorcerers, able to cast spells like a 1d6+6 level magic user.
