# Elf
**No. Appearing:** 3d4 (scouting party)<br>
**Armour Class:** 13 (leather)<br>
**Hit Dice:** 1<br>
**Attacks:** Sword 1d8 or Bow 1d6+1<br>
**Nat 19:** as weapon<br>
**Attributes:** S:10 D:15 C:8 I:10 P:13 W:10 Ch:13<br>
**Luck:** 4<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

Elves have the special qualities noted under the Race description. Culturally speaking, elves value freedom, beauty and the natural world, which sometimes brings them into conflict with expansionist humans and gold hungry dwarves. Elves can live for many human generations, giving them an aloofness and arrogance that promotes an isolationist view.
