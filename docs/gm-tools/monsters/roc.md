# **Roc**
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 13<br>
**Hit Dice:** 14<br>
**Attacks:** 2 x Claws 2d6 and Bite 2d10+2<br>
**Nat 19:** special<br>
**Attributes:** S:23 D:13 C:20 I:2 P:14 W:16 Ch:10<br>
**Luck:** 14<br>
**Move:** 240 ft flying<br>

Rocs are dragon sized hawk like birds, large enough to prey upon elephants. On a Claw attack roll of 19-20, the roc may grab the target in one claw and fly away with it (often to be dropped from a great height, then eaten, or fed to hatchlings). The target may use their action to make an opposed Str or Dex check to slip free. 15 ft reach.
