# Ghoul
**No. Appearing:** 2d12<br>
**Armour Class:** 13<br>
**Hit Dice:** 3<br>
**Attacks:** Claw 1d6+1 and Bite 1d4<br>
**Nat 19:** the target is subject to the equivalent of *Slow* for 1d6 rounds (*Luck (Con)* save resists).<br>
**Attributes:** S:16 D:13 C:12 I:10 P:13 W:14 Ch:8<br>
**Luck:** 6<br>
**Move:** 40 ft<br>

Ghoul are intelligent, fast paced pack-hunters that sustain their unnatural existence on a diet of corpses. They are *Undead* with all the usual benefits. A ghoul's touch carries powerful necromantic energy, requiring a *Luck (Con)* save or the victim is paralysed for 1d6 x 10 minutes. Ghouls will sometimes make an opportunistic retreat with a paralysed adventurer in tow, scampering back to their hidden lair before engaging in a feeding frenzy.
