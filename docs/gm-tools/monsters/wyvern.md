# Wyvern
**No. Appearing:** 1d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 17<br>
**Hit Dice:** 13<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 2d8 and Sting 1d6+2 + poison<br>
**Nat 19:** the target is grabbed and may be carried away.<br>
**Attributes:** S:22 D:16 C:20 I:3 P:15 W:14 Ch:7<br>
**Luck:** 13<br>
**Move:** 30 ft or 90 ft when flying<br>

Wyverns are 30 ft, two-legged quasi dragons, with horned skulls, and fore claws attached to their wings. They have a bestial intellect and cunning. Wyverns *Cause Injuries* on critical hits and have a 10 ft reach. A wyvern's stinger carries a potent poison causing 1d4 Str loss (*Luck* (Con) save for half). An adventurer has a 20% chance of harvesting 1d4 doses of poison from a dead wyvern.
