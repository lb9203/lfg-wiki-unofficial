# Horse
**No. Appearing:** Any<br>
**Armour Class:** 10<br>
**Hit Dice:** 2<br>
**Attacks:** Hoof 1d6<br>
**Nat 19:** the target is trampled for double damage and is knocked prone.<br>
**Attributes:** S:19 D:13 C:16 I:2 P:10 W:13 Ch:6<br>
**Luck:** 5<br>
**Move:** 60 ft<br>

The above stats are for a riding horse. Warhorses have AC 15, 3 HD and make a Hoof (1d8+1) attack. A horse acts at the same time as the rider but makes separate attacks. Riding horses will generally attempt to flee combat rather than fight. A war horse might require persuading to attack certain monsters.
