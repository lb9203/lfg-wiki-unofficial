# Falcon
**No. Appearing:** 1 or 2<br>
**Armour Class:** 14<br>
**Hit Dice:** 1d4 hp<br>
**Attacks:** Rake 1d3<br>
**Nat 19:** eyes bloodied, target blind until end of their next turn.<br>
**Attributes:** S:15 D:16 C:10 I:4 P:16 W:13 Ch:7<br>
**Luck:** 5<br>
**Move:** 120 ft<br>

The above stats are for a trained falcon, owl, raven or other medium to large sized bird. Advantage on sight based perception checks. Some birds see as well in darkness as light, provided there is some light to amplify (eg moonlight).
