# Bear
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 11<br>
**Hit Dice:** 4+4<br>
**Attacks:** 2 x Claws 1d6+1 and Bite 2d4<br>
**Nat 19:** bear hug<br>
**Attributes:** S:19 D:8 C:115 I:3 P:12 W:15 Ch:7<br>
**Luck:** 7<br>
**Move:** 40 ft<br>

Large bears stand up to 9 ft tall and weigh more than a 1,500 pounds. If hit with both claws, targets are caught in a crushing bear hug, requiring a roll on the *Injuries & Setbacks* table (*Luck* (Con) save resists).
