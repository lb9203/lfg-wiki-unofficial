# Wolf
**No. Appearing:** 3d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 12<br>
**Hit Dice:** 1+2<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 1d4+1<br>
**Nat 19:** the target is knocked prone.<br>
**Attributes:** S:13 D:14 C:15 I:3 P:12 W:10 Ch:6<br>
**Luck:** 4<br>
**Move:** 60 ft<br>

Wolves are pack hunters and may be found in large numbers. Male wolves weigh from 80 to 100 pounds and have advantage when tracking. If wolves flank their prey, they gain advantage on attack rolls instead of +1. Wolf stats might also substitute for hunting dogs, war dogs, and so on.
