# Tiger
**No. Appearing:** 2d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 13<br>
**Hit Dice:** 4<br>
**Attacks:** 2 x Claws 1d4+2 and Bite 1d8+1<br>
**Nat 19:** the target is knocked prone.<br>
**Attributes:** S:19 D:13 C:17 I:3 P:13 W:10 Ch:6<br>
**Luck:** 7<br>
**Move:** 50 ft<br>

Tigers have advantage when tracking and on stealth checks. A tiger that hits with both Claws knocks the target prone, and automatically hits with its bite.
