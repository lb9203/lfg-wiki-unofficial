# Chuul
**No. Appearing:** 1d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 16<br>
**Hit Dice:** 5+2<br>
**Attacks:** Claw 2d6 and Tentacle (poison, see below)<br>
**Nat 19:** the target is caught in the chuul's pincer, automatically suffering one Claw hit on the monster's turn. A Str contest is required to break free.<br>
**Attributes:** S:19 D:12 C:15 I:10 P:12 W:14 Ch:5<br>
**Luck:** 8<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

The chuul is an 8 ft tall, unnatural merging of arthropods spawned from another planet or dimension. They appear as crab like humanoids with tentacles where their mouths should be. Their carapace is incredibly tough, making chuuls immune to critical hits. The slime from a chuul's tentacle infects the target with a minor madness (a *Luck* (Will) save resists).
