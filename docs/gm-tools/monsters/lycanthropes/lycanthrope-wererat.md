# **Lycanthrope, Wererat**
**No. Appearing:** 4d6<br>
**Armour Class:** 12<br>
**Hit Dice:** 3<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 2d4<br>
**Nat 19:** if appropriate, a giant rat comes to the lycanthrope's aid.<br>
**Attributes:** S:14 D:18 C:10 I:13 P:14 W:10 Ch:8 (Hybrid)<br>
**Luck:** 6<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

Wererats are often found in cities, lurking in sewers or abandoned alleyways. By spending an action, they may exert control and issue orders to ordinary rats, giant rats and dire rats, which are compelled to obey. Wererats are extremely sneaky and gain advantage on Stealth checks.
