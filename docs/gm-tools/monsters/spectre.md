# Spectre
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 15<br>
**Hit Dice:** 7<br>
**Attacks:** Spectral weapon, or touch 1d8 + level drain---<br>
**Nat 19:** the Spectre and target vanish to a nightmare realm of dark sorcery to finish their duel. If the Spectre is destroyed, the target reappears.<br>
**Attributes:** S:- D:15 C:- I:13 P:15 W:15 Ch:9<br>
**Luck:** 9<br>
**Move:** 30 ft in any direction<br>

Spectres are *Incorporeal Undead* that pass through solid objects like water. A spectre's icy touch drains one level until the end of the adventure (a *Luck* (Will) save resists). An adventurer reduced to zero levels dies, and rises as a subservient wraith in 1d4 days. Spectres possess a malign intelligence and remember much of their former life, but those memories are tainted by feelings of hate, vengeance and torment.
