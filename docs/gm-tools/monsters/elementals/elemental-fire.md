# Elemental, Fire
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 16<br>
**Hit Dice:** 5, 10, or 15<br>
**Attacks:** Claw 2d6, 2d8 or 3d8<br>
**Nat 19:** the target's clothes catch fire, causing 1d6 hp damage each round until extinguished.<br>
**Attributes:** S:17/18/19 D:20 C:13 I:3 P:10 W:16 Ch:3<br>
**Luck:** 8, 10, or 15<br>
**Move:** 120 ft flying<br>

Fire Elementals rake their opponents with searing claws or blast them with 60 ft cones of flame 20 ft wide at the base causing 5d6, 10d6 or 15d6 damage (*Luck* (Dex) save for half). The Fire Blast attack has a 30% chance of recharging at the start of the elemental's turn. Creatures within 5 ft of a fire elemental have a 50% chance of suffering 1d6 fire damage on its turn. Fire elementals shed light according to their size, and cause flammable materials to ignite.
