# Elemental, Earth
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 19<br>
**Hit Dice:** 5, 10, or 15<br>
**Attacks:** Fist 2d6, 2d10 or 3d10<br>
**Nat 19:** the target is knocked prone and momentarily stunned, losing its next action (*Luck* (Con) save resists).<br>
**Attributes:** S:19/21/23 D:10 C:22 I:3 P:10 W:16 Ch:3<br>
**Luck:** 8, 10 or 15<br>
**Move:** 40 ft including when burrowing<br>

Earth elementals hammer or crush opponents with their mighty fists. They may merge with the earth, passing through it like water or burrowing through to leave a tunnel. 15 HD elementals *Cause Injuries* on a natural 19-20 attack roll.
