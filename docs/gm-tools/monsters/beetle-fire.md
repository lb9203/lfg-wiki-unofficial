# Beetle, Fire
**No. Appearing:** 2d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 16<br>
**Hit Dice:** 4<br>
**Attacks:** Acid Spray 2d4+2<br>
**Nat 19:** the target is caught in the Beetle's mandibles, automatically suffering 2d4 acid damage each turn. A Str contest is required to break free.<br>
**Attributes:** S:16 D:10 C:14 I:2 P:10 W:12 Ch:8<br>
**Luck:** 7<br>
**Move:** 30 ft or 15 ft burrowing<br>

Fire beetles are 4 ft carnivores with thick, luminescent shells. They burrow through soft earth at half their usual movement rate and often ambush their prey from below. Fire beetles automatically sense the movement of ground based creatures within 60 ft. They attack with a jet of acid that targets one creature up to 50 ft.
