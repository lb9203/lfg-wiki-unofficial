# **Sprite**
**No. Appearing:** 5d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 12<br>
**Hit Dice:** 1 hit point<br>
**Attacks:** Tiny Bow, or Blade 1d2 + poison<br>
**Nat 19:** the target is cursed and loses 1 point of *Luck*.<br>
**Attributes:** S:3 D:17 C:5 I:12 P:13 W:15 Ch:15<br>
**Luck:** 3<br>
**Move:** 30 ft including when flying or swimming<br>

Sprites are 6 inch fey humanoids often found in forests or near water. Land based sprites (aka pixies) have gossamer wings, while water sprites have gills and are amphibious. Sprites are intensely curious, jealous, vengeful and notoriously capricious. Sprite poison requires a *Luck* (Will) save or imposes a deep slumber for 1d4 hours. Upon awakening, the victim has no memory of the hour prior to the poison, and is often bereft of any shiny or beautiful trinkets such as coins, gems and jewellery. An adventurer has a 50% chance of looting 1d3 doses of poison from a band of sprites.
