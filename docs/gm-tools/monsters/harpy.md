# Harpy
**No. Appearing:** 2d6<br>
**Armour Class:** 12<br>
**Hit Dice:** 3<br>
**Attacks:** 2 x Talons 1d4<br>
**Nat 19:** the target must make a *Luck* (Will) save or suffer a minor madness.<br>
**Attributes:** S:9 D:15 C:10 I:10 P:12 W:13 Ch:15<br>
**Luck:** 6<br>
**Move:** 30 ft and 60 ft when flying<br>

Harpies have the upper body of a human female and the lower body and wings of a vulture. Their song is a charm that draws victims to the harpy (a *Luck* (Will) save resists). A harpy's touch enchants the victim like a *Charm Person* spell (even if injured and in combat) and drains 1d4 Str each day until death at zero Str (when the Harpy finally devours her meal).
