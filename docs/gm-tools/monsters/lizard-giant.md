# **Lizard, Giant**
**No. Appearing:** 1 or 2d6 if nesting<br>
**Armour Class:** 15<br>
**Hit Dice:** 3+4<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 2d6<br>
**Nat 19:** the giant lizard spits acid on the target, causing 1d4 damage for 1d6 rounds (action to automatically clean off).<br>
**Attributes:** S:17 D:10 C:14 I:2 P:8 W:10 Ch:3<br>
**Luck:** 6<br>
**Move:** 60 ft<br>

Giant Lizards are 12 ft carnivorous reptiles. They are highly territorial when nesting in broods. If raised from a young age, giant lizards can be ridden like mounts, making their eggs valuable in certain quarters.
