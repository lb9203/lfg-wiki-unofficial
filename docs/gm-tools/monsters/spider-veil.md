# **Spider, Veil**
**No. Appearing:** 1d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 15<br>
**Hit Dice:** 8+2<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 2d6 + poison<br>
**Nat 19:** the veil spider automatically phases out of existence after its turn is complete.<br>
**Attributes:** S:15 D:19 C:12 I:2 P:14 W:14 Ch:5<br>
**Luck:** 9<br>
**Move:** 40 ft and may climb walls, ceilings, etc<br>

Veil spiders are 8 ft long, natural ambushers who surprise their prey by phasing into existence from beyond the veil (automatic surprise at the GM's discretion). A veil spider's poisonous bite is lethal after 1d3 rounds on a failed *Luck* (Con) save. An apothecary may be able to delay the poison's effect for 1d3 hours (1d3 days on a great success). A veil spider has a 50% chance of phasing out of existence after its turn, reappearing at the start of its next turn anywhere within 40 ft.
