# Cockatrice
**No. Appearing:** 1d6<br>
**Armour Class:** 13<br>
**Hit Dice:** 4<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 1d3 + turn to stone<br>
**Nat 19:** the target's weapon or armour (50%) is petrified (Luck save resists).<br>
**Attributes:** S:6 D:17 C:11 I:2 P:13 W:10 Ch:5<br>
**Luck:** 7<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

The cockatrice is a 6 ft, black feathered wyvern shaped beast with bird legs and a spine crested rooster head. Its bite turns enemies to stone after 1d4 rounds unless the victim succeeds on a *Luck* (Con) save. Such petrification can be reversed with cockatrice blood, or a *Flesh to Stone* spell.
