# **Invisible Stalker**
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 16<br>
**Hit Dice:** 8<br>
**Attacks:** “Bite” 2d8<br>
**Nat 19:** the target and stalker vanish to the void to finish their duel. If the stalker is destroyed, the target reappears.<br>
**Attributes:** S:18 D:19 C:15 I:15 P:19 W:15 Ch:7<br>
**Luck:** 9<br>
**Move:** 40 ft flying<br>

Invisible stalkers are 7 ft supernatural assassins from the void, and are most commonly encountered as a result of the *Invisible Stalker* spell or wayward DDM check. They are invisible, winged monsters compelled to obey a single command from their conjurer until they succeed or are destroyed. Invisible stalkers have 50% *Magic Resistance*.
