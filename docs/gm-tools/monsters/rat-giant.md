# **Rat, Giant**
**No. Appearing:** 5d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 11<br>
**Hit Dice:** 1d4 hp<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 1d3<br>
**Nat 19:** If appropriate, another Giant Rat (60%) or Dire Rat (40%) comes to aid this Giant Rat.<br>
**Attributes:** S:3 D:14 C:8 I:2 P:13 W:6 Ch:4<br>
**Luck:** 3<br>
**Move:** 40 ft<br>

Giant rats are about the size of a large cat. They are often found in sewers and lurking in forests nearby civilization. A giant rat bite has a 50% chance of carrying a wasting illness (or other random disease) causing sufferers to lose 1d4 Str each day until they are bedridden at zero Str. A *Luck* (Con) save resists. An apothecary might cure the disease in 1d3 days with the right healing herbs.
