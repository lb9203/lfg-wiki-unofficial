# **Spider, Giant**
**No. Appearing:** 2d4<br>
**Armour Class:** 13<br>
**Hit Dice:** 2+2<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 1d6+1 + poison<br>
**Nat 19:** special<br>
**Attributes:** S:14 D:16 C:12 I:2 P:12 W:10 Ch:4<br>
**Luck:** 5<br>
**Move:** 40 ft and may climb walls, ceilings, etc<br>

Giant Spiders are 6 ft long, aggressive hunters and web builders. On a natural 19-20 attack roll, a giant spider wraps its victim in webs, rendering them helpless (a *Luck* (Str) save resists). A trapped victim may spend an action to attempt to break free (an opposed Str check vs Str 17). A giant spider's poisonous bite causes 1d6 damage and 1 Dex loss (a *Luck* (Con) save resists). An adventurer has a 25% chance of harvesting 1d3 doses of poison from a dead spider (may require an Int (Animal Lore) or Apothecary check).
