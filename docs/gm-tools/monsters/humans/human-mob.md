# **Human (Mob)**
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 10<br>
**Hit Dice:** 4<br>
**Attacks:** Flaming torch, pitchforks, etc 1d6<br>
**Nat 19:** an opposed Str check vs Str 14 is required to avoid being pinned down (helpless next turn).<br>
**Attributes:** S:15 D:6 C:10 I:7 P:10 W:10 Ch:7<br>
**Luck:** 5<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

Mobs generally consist of scores of angry armed commoners. Apply the *Swarm* rules (p.183). A rousing speech or display of overwhelming force might disperse or delay a mob (GM's call).
