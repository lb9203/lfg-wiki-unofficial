# **Human (Berserker)**
**No. Appearing:** 3d10 (raiders) or 3d100 (tribe)<br>
**Armour Class:** 13 (Heavy Hide)<br>
**Hit Dice:** 1<br>
**Attacks:** Axe 1d8+1<br>
**Nat 19:** as weapon<br>
**Attributes:** S:13 D:10 C:13 I:8 P:9 W:12 Ch:10<br>
**Luck:** 4<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

Berserkers are warriors that have learnt to fight with astounding ferocity. They gain a +2 bonus on attack rolls and never make morale checks once combat is joined. Some particularly fearsome berserkers have barbarian class levels.
