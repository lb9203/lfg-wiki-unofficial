# **T-Rex**
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 14<br>
**Hit Dice:** 20<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 3d10+2<br>
**Nat 19:** special<br>
**Attributes:** S:23 D:13 C:21 I:3 P:12 W:15 Ch:4<br>
**Luck:** 16<br>
**Special:** See below<br>
**Move:** 60 ft<br>

Tyrannosaurus Rex is the gigantic, 40 ft dinosaur king from the prehistoric era. Its oversized skull and jaws, in combination with its aggression and speed, usually make it the top predator in the area. Creatures of 4 HD or less must make a *Will* check or flee in terror for 2d6 rounds or until out of sight. **T-Rex** *Cause Injuries* and critical his on 19-20 attack rolls. They have a 15 ft reach.
