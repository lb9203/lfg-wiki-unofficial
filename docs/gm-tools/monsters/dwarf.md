# **Dwarf**
**No. Appearing:** 4d6 (mining party) or 2d100 + 300 (merchant clan)<br>
**Armour Class:** 15 (plate)<br>
**Hit Dice:** 1<br>
**Attacks:** Warhammer 1d4+2<br>
**Nat 19:** by weapon.<br>
**Attributes:** S:11 D:10 C:14 I:10 P:10 W:10 Ch:8<br>
**Luck:** 4<br>
**Move:** 30 ft<br>

Dwarves have the special qualities noted under the Races description. Culturally speaking, dwarves place a high value on honour, loyalty, and expert craftsmanship, and are renowned builders and miners. A dwarf puts his clan name before his own. Dwarves have a racial lust for gold and gems that most cannot resist.
