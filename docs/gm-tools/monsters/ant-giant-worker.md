# Ant, Giant (Worker)
**No. Appearing:** 2d6<br>
**Armour Class:** 13<br>
**Hit Dice:** 2<br>
**Attacks:** Bite 1d6<br>
**Nat 19:** The worker knocks the target prone.<br>
**Attributes:** S:16 D:10 C:16 I:1 P:10 W:10 Ch:4<br>
**Luck:** 5<br>
**Move:** 40 ft and may climb walls, ceilings etc<br>

These 3 ft worker ants live in hives and venture to the surface for food. Workers may swarm a target, gaining +1 to hit for each additional ant beyond the first (maximum +4).
