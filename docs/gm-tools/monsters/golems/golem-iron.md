# Golem, Iron
**No. Appearing:** 1<br>
**Armour Class:** 20<br>
**Hit Dice:** 15+7<br>
**Attacks:** 2 x Weapon or 2 x Fists 2d10<br>
**Nat 19:** special<br>
**Attributes:** S:22 D:12 C:24 I:- P:15 W:- Ch:-<br>
**Luck:** 15<br>
**Move:** 40 ft<br>

Iron golems are the greatest of the automatons; 16 ft animated statues of hardened metal. They attack with hammer like fists or gigantic weapons. Iron golems may breathe noxious gas in a 30 ft cone, 10 ft wide at the base, causing 10d6 damage (*Luck (Con)* save for half). The Breath Weapon has a 30% chance of recharging at the start of the golem's turn. They have 90% *Magic Resistance*, except against lightning which "slows" them (p.100), and fire which heals them. Iron golems have a 10 ft reach, make *Off Turn Attacks*, and *Cause Injuries* on natural attack rolls of 19-20.
