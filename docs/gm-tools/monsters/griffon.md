# **Griffon**
**No. Appearing:** 2d6<br>
**Armour Class:** 14<br>
**Hit Dice:** 4<br>
**Attacks:** 2 x Claws 1d4+1 and Bite 2d4<br>
**Nat 19:** special<br>
**Attributes:** S:19 D:15 C:16 I:2 P:14 W:13 Ch:8<br>
**Luck:** 7<br>
**Move:** 60 ft or 120 ft when flying<br>

Griffons have the body of a lion or horse, with the wings, head, and talons of an eagle. They usually nest in high mountain aeries, where they lay their eggs and hunt their prey. On a natural attack roll of 19-20, a humanoid target is grabbed and may be flown away with. The target may use their action to initiate an opposed Str check to break free.
