# Owlbear
**No. Appearing:** 1 or a mated pair<br>
**Armour Class:** 13<br>
**Hit Dice:** 6+3<br>
**Attacks:** 2 x Claws 1d6+2 and Bite 2d4<br>
**Nat 19:** bear hug (see below).<br>
**Attributes:** S:19 D:12 C:16 I:3 P:10 W:15 Ch:4<br>
**Luck:** 8<br>
**Move:** 40 ft<br>

Owlbears have the body of a bulky 10 ft bear and the head of an owl, including a powerful lacerated beak. Owlbears that hit with both claw attacks draw the victim into a crushing hug forcing the victim to roll on the *Injuries & Setbacks* table (a *Luck* (Con) save resists). Owlbears are foul tempered and explode into extreme violence; they cause 20 hp damage on critical hits and have a 10 ft reach.

A *Giant Ape* might substitute for owlbears in remote jungle areas, with Dex 14, a 40 ft climb speed, and throw boulders/branches for 2d6 damage up to 50 ft. *Winged Giant Apes* also have a 40 ft fly speed.
