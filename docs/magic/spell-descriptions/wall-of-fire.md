# Wall of Fire

4th Level<br>
Range: 120 ft<br>
Duration: 1d6 x 10 minutes

A wall of opaque fire flares into being, either a wall up to 60 ft long and 20 ft high, or a 15 ft radius half dome. The flames are two feet thick. Entering the wall causes 1d8 damage per two caster levels (*Luck* (Con) save for half). If conjured so that the wall touches a creature, a *Luck* (Dex) save negates any damageand the target chooses which side of the wall it ends up on.
