# Levitate

2nd Level<br>
Range: 60 ft<br>
Duration: 1d6 x 10 minutes

This spell allows the caster to levitate himself or another creature (up to 10 ft tall), or object (up to 10 kgs per level). The caster may spend their action to direct the target's movement in any direction at a rate of 10 ft per round, to a maximum height of 10 ft per level. An unwilling target or an attended object may make a *Luck* (Str) save to resist. Unattended magical objects have a 50% chance of resisting.
