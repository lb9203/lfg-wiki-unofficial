# Lightning Bolt

3rd Level<br>
Range: 120 ft<br>
Duration: Instantaneous

A 10 ft wide bolt of lightning blasts outward 120 ft from the caster. Targets in the path suffers 1d6 damage per caster level (*Luck* (Dex) save for half). Flammable objects in the area may catch fire. If outdoors under clouds, the bolt may descend from the sky instead, causing 1d8 damage per caster level.
