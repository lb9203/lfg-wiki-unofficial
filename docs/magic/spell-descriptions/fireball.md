# Fireball

3rd Level<br>
Range: 240 ft<br>
Duration: Instantaneous

The caster hurls a ball of fire which explodes at the target location with a furnace like blast. If the ball strikes something before reaching the intended destination, it detonates early. The blast radius is 20 ft, and causes 1d6 damage per caster level (*Luck* (*Dex*) save for half). The blast shapes itself to the available volume (33,000 cubic feet), filling 33 ten-by-ten-by-ten cubical areas. Flammable objects in the area catch fire.
