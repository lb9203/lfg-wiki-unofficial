# Disintegrate

6th Level<br>
Range: 60 ft<br>
Duration: Instant

The caster fires a beam of green energy at a single target such as an object or creature, which is instantly turned to microscopic dust, leaving no trace. If an object such as a wall is targeted, a maximum of a 10 ft cube is affected. Creatures may make a *Luck* (Dex) save to resist. Magical items generally have a 50% chance of resisting.
