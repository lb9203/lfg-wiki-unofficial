# Shield

1<sup>st</sup> Level<br>
Range: Self<br>
Duration: 2d6 hours or until expended

The caster is protected by a hovering disc of force that phases in and out of existence as needed. The disc protects the caster like a normal shield but does not require any hands. If the caster uses the shield ability to negate a physical attack (p.62), the spell ends. If the caster is subject to *Magic Missile*, the *Shield* automatically negates it, then ends.
