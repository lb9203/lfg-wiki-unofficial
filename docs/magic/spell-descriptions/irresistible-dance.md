# Irresistible Dance

6th Level<br>
Range: 120 ft<br>
Duration: 2d6 rounds

This spell causes a single target of up to 15 HD to compulsively and energetically dance on the spot (no *Luck* save is permitted). The target is helpless while dancing and unable to move normally or take other actions. When the spell ends the target collapses, utterly exhausted, and is befuddled with no memory of the prior 1d6 x 10 minutes. Creatures immune to charm effects are immune to this spell. *Cure Malady* ends the effect and restores any lost memory.
