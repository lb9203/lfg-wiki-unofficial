# Confusion

4th Level<br>
Range: 120 ft<br>
Duration: 1d4 hours

This spell affects up to 2d6 creatures within a 30 ft radius, causing them to stand dazed or manically attack others nearby. A *Luck* (Will) save resists. If the spell takes hold, roll for the effect every 10 minutes until the duration has run its course.
