# Forbidden Wish

6<sup>th</sup> Level<br>
Range: 240 ft<br>
Duration: Instant

This spell may mimic any other spell of 5<sup>th</sup> level or less, or bring into being another magical effect of approx 5<sup>th</sup> level power, subject to GM discretion. Alternatively, the caster may choose to boost the power of the spell beyond the usual 6<sup>th</sup> level limits by invoking a mysterious entity from another dimension. The GM decides what is possible and may require a bargain to be struck between the caster and entity. In such a case, once the spell is complete, the caster automatically triggers a *Dark & Dangerous Magic* effect, gains a pervasive madness, and suffers 5d4 Con loss (if

reduced to zero Con, the caster dies).
