# Cure Light Wounds

1st Level<br>
Range: Touch<br>
Duration: Immediate

If the target has 1 or more hit points, this spell instantly restores hit points equal to 2d4 + the caster's Int modifier. If the target is unconscious on zero hit points, the wounds mend slowly over 1d3 minutes. The target does not regain hit points or consciousness until the 1d3 minutes passes. A reversal of this spell causes the same damage with a touch (make a melee attack roll with advantage).
