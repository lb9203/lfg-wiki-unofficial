# Move Earth

6th Level<br>
Range: 240 ft<br>
Duration: 2d6 minutes

This spell allows the caster to spend an action to shift, move and shape earth and/or stone in a 10 ft diameter. The caster must be able to see the material affected. If the caster attempts to bury a humanoid opponent, the target must make a *Luck* (Dex) save or be trapped and suffocating. A suffocating target may spend their action to gain a 33% chance to break free.
