# Speak with Plants

4<sup>th</sup> Level<br>
Range: Self<br>
Duration: 1d6 x 10 minutes

The caster may engage in two way telepathic communication with plants (including plant monsters) within 60 ft. The spell imbues the caster's communications with persuasive influence, granting advantage on any Cha checks with plants. The caster may temporarily imbue any non-intelligent plant within 60 ft with a degree of magical flexibility. For example, a tree might move its branches to form a bridge, or vines untangle and wriggle aside.
