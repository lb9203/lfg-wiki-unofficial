# Stoneskin

4<sup>th</sup> Level<br>
Range: Self<br>
Duration: 2d6 rounds

Whenever the caster is subject to physical damage of any kind (including magical damage), his skin becomes unnaturally hard, resisting part of the blow. Each time damage is suffered, the amount is reduced to 1d100 percent (eg: on a roll of 43, the caster suffers 43% of the damage rolled).
