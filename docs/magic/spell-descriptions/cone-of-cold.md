# Cone of Cold

5th Level<br>
Range: 60 ft cone with a 20 ft base<br>
Duration: instant

This spell blasts foes with freezing hoarfrost, hammering them with ice and numbing cold. Targets suffer 1d8 damage per caster level, with a *Luck* (Dex) save reducing damage by half. Targets that fail their *Luck* save are also semi-frozen, unable to move from their location. A character may attempt a Str check (as part of their move action) to shake off this secondary effect each turn.
