# Contact Other Plane

5<sup>th</sup> Level<br>
Range: None<br>
Duration: 1d10 minutes

The caster makes contact with a supernatural entity mankind was not meant to know, and may force a yes or no answer to questions posed. The caster may ask up to 5 questions, but each has a 20% chance (cumulative) of inflicting a serious madness. The entity communed with answers the questions truthfully, but may answer cryptically or in riddles (GM discretion).
