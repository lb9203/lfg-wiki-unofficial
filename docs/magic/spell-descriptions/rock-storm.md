# Rock Storm

4th Level<br>
Range: 120 ft<br>
Duration: Special

A swirling vortex of rocks hammers a 15 ft radius,

inflicting 1d6 damage per caster level (*Luck* (Con) save for half). The area becomes slippery with scree for 1d4 rounds. Anyone moving more than half their normal movement rate must make a Dex check or fall prone.
