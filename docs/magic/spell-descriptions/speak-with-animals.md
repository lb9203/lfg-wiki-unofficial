# Speak with Animals

2nd Level<br>
Range: Self<br>
Duration: 1d6 x 10 minutes

This enchantment causes normal animals and insects (not *Giant* or *Dire* versions) within 60 ft to regard the caster as friendly unless her actions suggest otherwise. The caster may engage in two way conversations with any animal and/or insect within range, during which time the caster makes chittering, barking, squawking, and similar noises. The spell imbues the caster's communications with persuasive influence, granting advantage on any Cha checks with animals and insects.
