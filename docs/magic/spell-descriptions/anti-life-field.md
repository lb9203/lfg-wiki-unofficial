# Anti-Life Field

5<sup>th</sup> Level<br>
Range: Self<br>
Duration: 1d6 x 10 minutes

An invisible force surrounds and moves with the caster in a 5 ft radius that repels other living creatures. Living creatures cannot pass through the globe. The globe can be used to pin a creature against another surface but cannot crush or otherwise injure it (attempting to do so ends the spell).
