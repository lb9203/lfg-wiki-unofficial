# Cloudkill

5th Level<br>
Range: 20 ft<br>
Duration: 2d6 x 10 minutes

The caster conjures a 15 ft radius cloud of poisonous and corrosive vapour. The cloud rolls forward at a move rate of 10 ft per round. The cloud is heavier than air and will flow down hills or stairs. Any creature caught in the cloud must make a *Luck* (Con) save or be incapacitated, unable to take actions or move out of the cloud. Each subsequent round in the cloud requires a *Luck* (Con) save to avoid a horrible, gurgling death.
