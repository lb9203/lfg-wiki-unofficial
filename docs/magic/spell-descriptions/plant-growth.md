# Plant Growth

4th Level<br>
Range: 240 ft<br>
Duration: Permanent

Over 1d6 minutes an area up to 100 ft long, 100 ft wide and 10 ft tall grows into an impassable forest of brush, thorns and vines.

The plants spring from any surface, including earth, stone, ice or snow and do not require sunlight or water to persist. An experienced woodsman armed with an axe takes approximately 1 minute to clear 5 ft of forest.
