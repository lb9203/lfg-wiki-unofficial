# Dispel Magic

3rd Level<br>
Range: 120 ft<br>
Duration: Instant or 2d6 rounds against a magical item.

This invocation is used to instantly end all spells and other magical effects in a 15 ft radius, including magical traps, locks and summoned creatures (which vanish). Alternatively, the spell can also be used to suspend the magical properties of a single magical item for 2d6 rounds. The caster must make a successful *Luck* (Int) check for the spell to work.
