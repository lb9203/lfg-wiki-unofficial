# Legend Lore

6th Level<br>
Range: Self<br>
Duration: See below

Over the course of a long ritual (5d20 days), the caster gains detailed knowledge of a person, place, or thing. There is a 50% chance of gaining access to particularly important secrets such as hidden lairs, concealed crimes, true identities, true names or prophecies. At the GM's discretion, some information may be cryptic in nature. This spell cannot be repeated against the same target. Over the course of casting this spell, the magic user gains a serious madness.
