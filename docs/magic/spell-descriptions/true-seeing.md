# True Seeing

6th Level<br>
Range: Touch<br>
Duration: 1d6 x 10 minutes

The target gains supernatural clarity of vision, seeing through all darkness and obscurement, detecting all forms of magic, invisibility, and illusion. If the subject is being magically observed, she becomes aware of such. Finally, the subject senses the true nature of any monster she observes (for example a dragon in human form, etc).
