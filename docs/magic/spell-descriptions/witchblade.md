# NA

2nd Level<br>
Range: Touch<br>
Duration: 2d6 rounds.

The caster touches a weapon no larger than a polearm, instilling it with temporary magic. The weapon counts as magical for the duration, and grants a random effect as long as it is wielded (roll 1d6).

<table border="1"><thead><tr><th>1d6</th><th>EFFECT</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>1</td><td><i>Elemental:</i> Wreathed in flame, ice, acid, etc. Increase damage die by one size category.</td></tr><tr><td>2</td><td><i>Lucky:</i> Increase critical range by 1.</td></tr><tr><td>3</td><td><i>Swift:</i> Gain advantage on Initiative checks.</td></tr><tr><td>4</td><td><i>Vampiric:</i> Wielder restores 1d4 hp on each hit.</td></tr><tr><td>5</td><td><i>Strength:</i> gain 1d4 Str (max 19).</td></tr><tr><td>6</td><td><i>Souleater:</i> Auto trigger a DDM effect if a target is killed.</td></tr></tbody></table>
