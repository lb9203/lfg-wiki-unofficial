# Blade Barrier

6th Level<br>
Range: Self<br>
Duration: 2d6 rounds

Flying magical blades encircle and move with the caster in a 15 ft radius. Creatures caught in the area when the spell is first cast suffer 6d12 damage (*Luck* (Dex) save for half). In subsequent rounds, creatures that enter the area or end their turn there, suffer the same damage. The caster's movement rate is reduced to 10 ft while the spell persists.
