# Magic Missile

1st Level<br>
Range: 60 ft<br>
Duration: Immediate

This spell conjures one tendril of life leeching energy for every odd caster level. The tendril(s) stretch from the caster's hands and automatically strike one or more visible creatures (not objects). Each lash causes 1d4+1 damage. *Magic Missile* is negated by a target protected by the *Shield* spell.
