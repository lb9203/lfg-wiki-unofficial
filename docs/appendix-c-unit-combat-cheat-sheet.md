## Movement

A engaged unit cannot move until the enemy unit is destroyed or broken. Moving to engage a unit other than the closest enemy requires a *Move check*. If a unit intends to move into or pass through difficult terrain, the GM may call for a *Move* check.

## Broken Units

A unit that becomes *Broken* immediately moves to an adjacent location with the fewest enemies. For the remainder of the mass battle, the *Broken* unit suffers disadvantage on all tests, and attacks against it gain advantage. A *Broken* unit that destroys an enemy unit is restored to normal status.
