# Weapons

Every adventurer needs at least one reliable weapon by her side, preferably more. Example weapons of average quality and price are provided below. Weapons made from special materials cost substantially more, sometimes up to 100 times as much.

## Special Metals

<table border="1"><thead><tr><th>METAL</th><th>SPECIAL QUALITY</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Adamantine</td><td>Cannot be seriously damaged by mundane means. +2 Str bonus when breaking things.</td></tr><tr><td>Cold Iron</td><td>+1 to hit and damage against <i>Demons</i> and <i>Undead</i>, and bypasses their immunities and/or resistances.</td></tr><tr><td>Mithral</td><td>+2 initiative bonus. Doubles throwing weapon distance or may throw other single handed weapons (eg longsword) up to 30 ft.</td></tr><tr><td>Silvered</td><td>+1 to hit and damage against <i>Lycanthropes</i> and <i>Aberrant Terrors</i>, and bypasses their immunities and/or resistances.</td></tr></tbody></table>

*Silvered* weapons are a silver-steel alloy useful against *Lycanthropes* and *Aberrant Terrors*. Weapons forged of *cold iron* (the oldest and purest iron ore, mined deep and sometimes exhibiting mild ferromagnetism) are best against *Demons* and *Undead*. *Mithral* is a fey metal, incredibly light and flexible. *Adamantine* is the hardest metal known to man, impervious to mundane forces once tempered.

An item cannot combine metal properties. There is something about the forging process that nullifies any prior property.

## Weapon Properties

Note some weapons cause a special effect on a natural 1d20 roll of 19. The GM may permit adventurers (and possibly some rare NPCs) to avoid this effect by making a successful *Luck* save. Most melee weapons require the attacker to be within 5 ft of their opponent, but some weapons have a 10 ft reach (ie striking range, as identified in the table).

## Ranged Weapons

Ranged weapons allow the user to attack from a safe distance. Ranged weapons and ammunition may be forged of special metals, just like melee weapons.

If the GM chooses to track ammunition, there is 50% chance of retrieving each piece used (90% for special metals). Ranged attacks may be made up to twice the listed range but suffer disadvantage beyond standard range.
