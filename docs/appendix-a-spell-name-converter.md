!!! note "Designer's Thoughts: Missing Magic?"

    Certain kinds of magic are absent from the default LFG spell lists, namely: resurrection magic, long range teleportation, thought/lie detection, and alignment detection (LFG does not use an alignment system).

    These kinds of spells tend to undermine some of the mystery of the setting, or bypass investigation or exploration. "Death is permanent" is also an important low magic trope, consistent with a gritty baseline world.
